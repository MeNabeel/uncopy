## Key Factors That Determine Your Loan Interest Rate

Lenders evaluate several factors to assign your personalized APR rate:

1. **Credit Score**: Borrowers with scores above 750 receive the lowest advertised interest rates (typically 8.99% - 11.50%).
2. **Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)**: Lenders prefer a DTI below 36%, meaning less than 36% of your monthly gross income goes toward existing debt payments.
3. **Employment Stability**: Permanent salaried employees at recognized corporations often qualify for lower processing fees.

<Callout type="warning" title="Watch Out For Hidden Fees">
Always check the loan contract for:
- Processing Fees (typically 1% to 3% of principal)
- Pre-payment / Foreclosure Charges (2% to 5% penalty if paid early)
- Late Payment Interest Surcharges
</Callout>

## When Does a Personal Loan Make Sense?

A personal loan is an effective financial tool when used for:
- **High-Interest Debt Consolidation**: Replacing credit card debt at 24% APR with a single personal loan at 10% APR.
- **Urgent Unforeseen Expenses**: Medical bills or emergency home repairs where liquid savings are insufficient.

Avoid taking personal loans for speculative investments, discretionary vacations, or non-essential luxury purchases.
