## What Makes a Great Student Credit Card?

When evaluating student credit cards, look past flashy sign-up bonuses and focus on terms that support smart financial habits:

1. **Zero Annual Fee**: You should never pay a fee to build your credit foundation.
2. **Cash Back Rewards**: Earn 1% to 5% cash back on everyday spending like groceries, dining out, and streaming platforms.
3. **Credit Bureau Reporting**: Ensure the card reports monthly to all three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion).
4. **Fraud Protection**: Standard $0 fraud liability protection against unauthorized charges.

<Callout type="tip" title="Building Credit Fast">
Keep your credit utilization ratio under 10%. If your credit limit is $1,000, try not to carry a statement balance higher than $100 before paying it off.
</Callout>

## How to Apply & Get Approved

Applying for a student card is straightforward, but make sure you have the following ready:

- Valid student ID or proof of enrollment (e.g., course schedule)
- Social Security Number or ITIN
- Proof of income (part-time job, scholarships, grants, or parental allowance)

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Carrying a Balance">
Credit card interest accumulates daily on unpaid balances. If you cannot pay the full balance, pay at least the minimum amount before the due date to avoid late payment penalties and damage to your credit score.
</Callout>
