## Reward Point Economics & Valuation

The core strength of the card lies in its **EDGE Reward Points**. Unlike standard cash back cards, transferring point balances to partner airline miles (such as Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, United MileagePlus, or Accor Live Limitless) unlocks enhanced point valuation.

## Is the Annual Fee Worth It?

If you spend over $15,000 annually on travel, dining, and international transactions, the combined value of lounge access, point transfers, and welcome vouchers easily surpasses the annual fee.

However, for casual spenders who prefer simple cash back without tracking mileage redemption charts, a zero-fee card will provide better net value.
